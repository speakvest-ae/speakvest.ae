Is your off-plan money safe?

Ask a question

Dubai's escrow system is one of the strongest protections for off-plan buyers in the region, but it only protects money that actually goes into the right account. Here's how it works and what to check.

1. How escrow protects you

  • Each project has its own escrow account at an approved bank, registered with the Land Department.
  • Your money can only be used to build your project.
  • Funds are released in stages against construction progress, verified by engineers.
  • A retention (typically 5%) is held for a year after completion.
  • If a project stalls, the authorities step in to complete it or refund buyers.

2. Before you book

  • Look up the developer and the project in the Dubai REST app.
  • Ask for the escrow account details in writing and compare them with the app.
  • Check your broker's BRN card.
  • Read the SPA: price, plan, handover date, delay and resale clauses.

3. When you pay

  • Pay only into the project's escrow account, never cash or a personal account.
  • Keep every receipt, in the project's name.
  • Make sure your purchase is registered on Oqood and ask for the certificate.

4. During construction

  • Compare the completion percentage in the app with each payment milestone.
  • Save developer updates and handover notices.

5. If it goes wrong

Contact the developer in writing, then the Land Department / RERA. Keep your SPA, receipts and Oqood certificate together, and take legal advice if needed.
