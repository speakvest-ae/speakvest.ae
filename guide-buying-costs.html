Buyer's guide · 2026

The real cost of buying in Dubai

Ask a question

The listing price is where your budget starts. Use this checklist to see every cost from offer to keys, and every year after.

1. Government fees (Dubai)

FeeTypical amountWho pays
DLD transfer fee4% of the priceBuyer (by convention)
Title deed / adminAbout AED 580 (apartments), more for landBuyer
Registration trusteeAED 4,000 + VAT (AED 2,000 + VAT under AED 500K)Buyer
Off-plan Oqood registration4% of the price + admin, at bookingBuyer (sometimes waived by developers)

2. Agency

On a resale, a buyer's agent usually charges 2% of the price + 5% VAT. Buying off-plan directly from the developer usually has no buyer's commission.

3. Mortgage costs

CostTypical amount
Mortgage registration0.25% of the loan + about AED 290
ValuationAbout AED 2,500 – 3,500
Arrangement feeUp to 1% of the loan
Life & property insuranceMonthly, set by the bank

4. Resale extras

  • Developer NOC fee (about AED 500 – 5,000 + VAT)
  • Mortgage release fees if the seller has a loan
  • Snagging inspection (optional, recommended)

5. Every year after

  • Service charges, per sq ft (check the building's figure published by DLD)
  • Chiller / district cooling, if not included
  • Home insurance
  • Property management, typically 5–8% of the rent, if letting
  • Holiday-home permits and fees, if letting short-term
